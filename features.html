Features

One platform, every part of the operation

From the first order to the last mile, and from a driver's phone to your books.

Dispatch board screenshot
Dispatch

A dispatch board built for daily use

Create trips from open orders and assign a driver, truck, and trailer in a few clicks. Every trip shows its stops, status, and documents in one place, so dispatchers aren't juggling spreadsheets and phone calls to answer "where's this load right now?"

  • Drag-and-drop driver, truck, and trailer assignment
  • Multi-stop trips with automatic distance calculation
  • Cross-border loads with PARS/PAPS and customs status tracked inline
Live trip tracking screenshot
Tracking

Know where every trip stands, in real time

Trip status updates as drivers move through their stops, so dispatch and customer service are always looking at the same current picture — not yesterday's spreadsheet or a call to the driver's cell.

  • Live status per stop: en route, arrived, loaded, delivered
  • Location shared from the driver's phone while on an active load
  • A shared view dispatch, ops, and customer-facing teams can all trust
Billing & invoicing screenshot
Billing

Invoicing that starts the moment a load delivers

Rates, accessorials, and driver settlements are tied to the load from the start, so invoicing isn't a separate reconciliation project at month-end — it's ready as soon as delivery is confirmed.

  • Auto-generate invoices from delivered loads
  • Driver settlements calculated from actual trip data
  • A clear billing history per customer and per tenant
Driver mobile app screenshot
Driver App

Everything a driver needs, on their own phone

No separate ELD hardware or in-cab tablet required to get started. Drivers log in, see their assigned trips, and move loads through status updates from a simple mobile app.

  • Assigned trips and stop details, always up to date
  • One-tap status updates from pickup to delivery
  • Location shared with dispatch only while a load is active
Admin & multi-tenant controls screenshot
Admin Controls

Run one fleet or many, from one account

Multi-tenant support means brokers and larger carriers can separate divisions, subsidiaries, or client accounts cleanly — each with their own users, data, and settings, without needing a separate login for every entity.

  • Role-based access for dispatchers, admins, and drivers
  • Each tenant's data kept separate, enforced platform-wide
  • Central billing and oversight across every tenant you manage
